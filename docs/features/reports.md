# Reports

Inkvoice includes built-in reports for tracking revenue, taxes, and outstanding payments. All reports can be exported as CSV.

To hand financial data to an accountant, see [Accounting Export](./accounting-export) — QuickBooks Online- and Xero-compatible CSV for invoices, payments and expenses.

## Available Reports

### Tax Summary

Shows collected tax amounts grouped by tax rate over a date range. Useful for tax filing.

- Filter by date range
- Grouped by tax definition (e.g., "VAT 20%", "GST 10%")
- Shows taxable amount and tax collected

### Aging Report

Shows outstanding invoices grouped by how overdue they are:

- **Current** — Not yet due
- **1-30 days** — Slightly overdue
- **31-60 days** — Moderately overdue
- **61-90 days** — Significantly overdue
- **90+ days** — Severely overdue

### Revenue by Customer

Breaks down total revenue by customer over a date range. Shows which clients contribute the most to your business.

### Revenue by Product

Breaks down total revenue by product or service. Shows which offerings generate the most income.

## Dashboard

The main dashboard provides at-a-glance metrics:

- **Total revenue** — Sum of all paid invoices
- **Outstanding** — Sum of unpaid sent invoices
- **Overdue** — Sum of overdue invoices
- **Invoice counts** — By status
- **Revenue chart** — Monthly revenue over time
- **Recent invoices** — Latest invoice activity
