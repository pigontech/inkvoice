# Features

Inkvoice covers the full invoicing workflow for freelancers and small businesses.

## Core

- **[Invoices](/features/invoices)** — Create, send, track, and manage invoices through their full lifecycle
- **[Quotes](/features/quotes)** — Send quotes to clients, then convert accepted quotes to invoices
- **[Customers](/features/customers)** — Manage your client directory with contact details and tax IDs
- **[Products](/features/products)** — Maintain a catalog of products and services with pricing, categories, and units

## Billing

- **[Recurring Invoices](/features/recurring)** — Set up automatic invoice generation on a schedule
- **[Online Payments](/features/payments)** — Accept Stripe payments directly from invoice links
- **Tax Management** — Define tax rates (VAT, GST, etc.) and apply them automatically to line items

## Tools

- **[Templates](/features/templates)** — Customize invoice appearance with Mustache HTML templates
- **[Reports](/features/reports)** — Tax summaries, aging reports, and revenue breakdowns
- **PDF Generation** — Generate and download professional PDFs from any invoice or quote
- **Email Sending** — Send invoices and quotes directly from the app via SMTP
- **Activity Log** — Track all changes across invoices, customers, and settings

## Platform

- **Multi-user** — Role-based access control with granular permissions
- **Multi-language** — English and Turkish built-in, extensible to any language
- **Dark mode** — System-aware theme with manual toggle
- **Mobile responsive** — Full functionality on any screen size
- **CSV Export** — Export invoices, customers, and products
- **Database Backup** — One-click database backup and download
